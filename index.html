What homeowners ask us

What areas do you serve?

We serve homes and cottages within about a one-hour radius of Perth, Ontario, including Carleton Place, Smiths Falls, Sharbot Lake, Lanark, Westport and surrounding communities. Not sure if you're in range? Just ask.

Do you install asphalt shingle roofs?

No. We specialize in premium steel roofing, which lasts far longer and performs better in Ontario winters. We do not install asphalt shingles.

What size projects do you take on?

We focus on larger, quality-driven work such as additions, full renovations, steel roofing, siding and decks, typically for homeowners investing $100,000 or more in their property.

How do I get a quote?

Fill out our project form with your vision and approximate budget, email sales@marlanesupply.com, or call 226-749-4980. We'll arrange an on-site walkthrough and provide a detailed estimate.

Do you build docks?

We build custom decks, not docks. If you're planning outdoor living space around your home or cottage, we'd love to help.

Our story

Quality is the whole point

MarLane Contracting was built around one idea: the work should speak for itself. We're a general contractor serving cottages and homes across the Rideau Lakes region, specializing in exterior and interior improvements that add real, lasting value.

We focus on the projects where craftsmanship matters most: steel roofing, additions, decks, siding, soffit & fascia, and windows. These aren't jobs you want to redo. They're investments in your home, and we treat them that way, from the first walkthrough to the final detail.

Most of our business comes from referrals and repeat clients, and that's exactly how we like it. When you do quality work, you don't have to chase the next job; it comes to you.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 August 2026

MarLane Contracting ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through our website at marlanecontracting.com, how we use it, and your choices.

Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us. When you use our contact or project form, we collect your name, email address, phone number, town or area, the service you're interested in, your approximate budget, and the details you share about your project.

Our website does not require you to create an account. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare a quote or estimate
  • To contact you about your project by email or phone
  • To keep records of our communications and work

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

How your information is sent

Our project form opens your own email application and sends your details directly to sales@marlanesupply.com. Your message travels through your email provider under their terms and privacy practices.

Cookies and analytics

This website uses only the cookies required for basic functionality. If we add analytics or advertising tools in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent.

Data retention

We keep enquiry information only as long as needed to serve you and to meet our legal and business record-keeping obligations, after which it is securely deleted.

Your rights

Under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA), you may request access to the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. To make a request, contact us at sales@marlanesupply.com or 226-749-4980.

Third-party links

Our site may link to other websites (for example, Google Maps). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows when it was last revised.
